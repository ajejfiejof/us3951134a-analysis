## Technical Field
Detecting, measuring or recording for diagnostic purposes by means of electric currents or magnetic fields; Measuring using microwaves or radio waves

## Core Invention
The patent discloses an apparatus and method for **remotely sensing and potentially altering brain wave activity without physical contact**. The fundamental principle:

1. **Transmit two electromagnetic signals** of different frequencies simultaneously to the brain
   - Example: 100 MHz and 210 MHz
   
2. **Signals interfere within brain tissue**, creating an "interference waveform" whose frequency equals the **difference** between the two transmitted frequencies
   - 110 MHz = 210 MHz - 100 MHz

3. **Brain wave activity modulates** this interference waveform

4. **The modulated signal is re-transmitted** by the brain and received by an antenna at a remote station

5. **Demodulated and processed** to produce a profile of the subject's brain waves

6. **Optionally, a compensating signal** can be transmitted back to the brain to alter neurological activity

## Key Components (from specification)
- High frequency transmitter producing two signals
- RF power divider
- Frequency doubler and oscillator (10 MHz, 100 MHz)
- Mixer circuits (sum and difference frequencies)
- Band pass filters
- Circulator/duplexer to isolate transmitted from received signals
- Receiver/amplifier/demodulator chain
- Computer for processing and analysis
- Visual display apparatus
- Auxiliary transmitter for compensating signals

## Example Embodiment
- 100 MHz signal and 210 MHz signal transmitted simultaneously
- Resultant 110 MHz interference wave modulated by brain activity
- 10 MHz intermediate frequency after mixing 110 MHz with 100 MHz
- Demodulated for display and computer processing

## Claims (11 claims)
1. Brain wave monitoring apparatus with means for producing base frequency, first signal, transmitting both, receiving response, mixing, and interpreting
2. Isolation of transmitted signals from received signals
3. Band pass filter connection
4. Amplifying response signal
5. Demodulating amplified response
6. Interpreting demodulated output
7. Producing electromagnetic control signal from response
8. Directing control signal to predetermined brain part
9. Process for monitoring brain wave activity (transmitting, receiving, interpreting)
10. Transmitting further electromagnetic wave to vary brain activity
11. Obtaining standard signal, comparing, producing compensating signal, transmitting to brain

## Potential Applications (from specification)
- Monitoring drivers/pilots for failure detection
- Detecting seizures, sleepiness, dreaming
- Monitoring pulse, heartbeat, hallucinations
- Medical diagnoses from remote stations
- Closed-loop compensating system for neurological response modification
